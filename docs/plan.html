01

Business ek nazar mein

Software ka focus: Inventory + Ledgers. Sale usi ka ek hissa hai.

VENDOR → raw material (kapda / board / foam …) khareedna → RAW MATERIAL STOCK (godown) → THEKEDAAR / KARIGAR ko material issue (Job banega) → ready maal wapas → FINISHED GOODS STOCK → SALE (retail + wholesale)

Saath-saath vendor ka paisa, karigar ka paisa aur stock — sab ek jagah, roz ke reports ke saath.

Purchase — vendor se raw material

Ek entry: Vendor · Date · Bill No. (optional)

Items table: har row → Item + Color + Unit + Qty + Rate + Amount → Total

Payment abhi: advance diya (optional) → baaki balance

Effect: raw stock badhta hai · vendor ledger (amount = dena, payment = kam)

Karigar Jobs — software ka dil

Job banao (material issue)

Job No. (auto) · Karigar · Date · issued material: Item + Color + Unit + Qty

Maal receipt (thoda-thoda bhi chalega)

Date · Finished product + variant + Qty · bacha material wapas aaya to note

Payment

Poore order ka thekedaar ko payment (labour)

Effect: raw stock kam (issued) · finished stock badhta hai (received) · karigar ledger · “kitna diya vs kitna aaya” apne aap.

Sale — retail + wholesale

Mobile number daalo → customer auto-find / auto-create · Date · Type: Retail / Wholesale

Items table: Product + Variant + Qty + Price (har item ke saamne, us waqt haath se) → Amount → Total

Effect: finished stock kam · Cash default, udhaar sirf regular customer

Payments

  • Vendor ko — advance / final settlement
  • Karigar ko — order payment
  • Customer se — udhaar wapas (receipt)
